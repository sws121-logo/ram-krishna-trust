About Our Trust

Our Mission

Ram Krishna Educational and Social Welfare Trust was established on 4th March 2015 with the vision to serve society through education, healthcare, and social welfare initiatives. Our mission is to empower communities and create sustainable development.

Our Activities

Education

Providing educational resources, books distribution in 126 schools, and supporting educational initiatives for underprivileged children.

Healthcare

Operating a hospital since 2020, free corona checking campaigns, and donations to Nawada Sadar Hospital.

Social Services

Government scheme facilitation, women hospitality, employment generation, and community support programs.

Agriculture & Cow Shelter

Supporting agricultural initiatives and maintaining cow shelters for community welfare.

Employment Generation

Creating employment opportunities through various initiatives including fishing business support.

Community Building

Connecting 70-75 field workers to implement various welfare programs and government schemes.

Making a Difference Since 2015

Our trust has been actively working towards community development through various initiatives in education, healthcare, and social welfare.
